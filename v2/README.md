# v2 — "One Flame" (Khodaldham · BS9 News · Wayonaa EV)

2:16 film, 1920×1080 / 30 fps, cut to **"Soul Instrumental Garba" by KalsStockMedia (Pixabay licence)**.
One spark is the hero from the first frame to the last; each brand is one act of the song.

| Song section | Time | Story |
|---|---|---|
| Intro | 0–11 s | A spark is born on a hairline, draws a diya and becomes its flame — "Every year, one flame / એક જ્યોત" |
| Groove 1 | 11–41 s | Garba rings orbit the flame ("LAKHS OF HEARTS", "one rhythm", "ગરબે ઘૂમે ગુજરાત") → the spark draws the Khodaldham temple of Kagvad (159 ft, flag) → writes the Khodaldham logo, which turns into the Gujarati wordmark |
| Break | 41 s | Only the spark remains; it turns blue |
| Groove 2 | 42–71 s | The spark is a light on Earth → pull out to the globe → dive to Gujarat → Rajkot lock → the city's roads carry data to it → broadcast rings, BREAKING / સમાચાર → the spark draws the BS9 ring, the logo lands on the big hit |
| Silence | 71–72 s | Black; the spark breathes and turns green |
| Groove 3 | 72–102 s | The spark draws an electric scooter (blueprint, 80 km range) and becomes its headlight → ride with G-RAZOR / G-ONE / G-LITE → battery charges → Wayonaa logo |
| Breakdown | 102–110 s | એક જ્યોત. one flame. / એક શહેર. one city. / એક ધબકાર. one heartbeat. |
